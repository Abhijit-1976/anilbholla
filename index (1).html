Leadership Coaching

Lead with clarity.

Leading at the top can be lonely. Decisions are bigger, people expect more, and there is little time to think. Coaching gives you a private space to think clearly, with someone who has led teams and carried results.

Who this is for

  • Senior leaders who want to lead better
  • Managers stepping into bigger roles
  • Founders and business owners
  • Leaders facing a change or a difficult decision

What we work on

  • Making better decisions under pressure
  • Leading and growing your people
  • Handling difficult conversations
  • Balancing work and personal life
  • Preparing for your next role

My approach

I listen first. I ask questions that help you find your own answer. I do not give speeches or ready-made formulas. I bring my coaching training and a career across India and abroad, across banking, financial services and startups, so we stay practical. I have coached for over 1,000 hours, one to one.

How we work

  • A free first conversation
  • Regular one to one sessions, in person in Mumbai or online
  • Action between sessions, reviewed at the next one

Separate from coaching

Mentoring

Coaching helps you find your own answers. Mentoring is different. Here I share what I have learned, so you can learn from my experience. I have mentored for over 250 hours.

I have worked in India and abroad, across different cultures and ways of doing business. I have worked across functions in banking and financial services, and in a startup, and then I built a business from scratch. This suits leaders who want a guide who has seen both large institutions and young companies.

Mentoring is offered separately, and we agree at the start which one you need. When both help, we keep them in separate sessions.

Life Guidance

Find your next step with clarity.

There are times when work, family or a big decision leaves you unsure which way to go. Life Guidance is a calm, private conversation to help you think it through and decide for yourself.

Who this is for

  • People at a turning point in career or life
  • Those preparing for retirement or a new stage
  • Professionals who feel stuck or unsure
  • Anyone who wants clarity before an important decision

What we talk about

  • Choices about work and career
  • Balancing family and personal goals
  • Building confidence for a change
  • Finding purpose in the next stage of life

My approach

I listen without judging. I ask simple questions that help you see your own situation clearly. The answers stay yours. I bring my coaching training and the experience of a long career and a full life.

How we work

  • A free first conversation
  • One to one sessions, in person in Mumbai or online
  • Complete privacy

What this is not

Life Guidance is not medical or mental health treatment. If you need that, I will gladly suggest you speak to a qualified doctor or therapist.
